## Prompt

Design a premium bilingual website concept for **Cejas Internacionales**, a beauty and micropigmentation brand led by Xiomara.

The website must feel elegant, professional, warm and international. It should not look like a generic beauty template, beige spa landing page, ecommerce store, or AI-generated card grid.

### Brand

- Brand name: Cejas Internacionales
- Main color: classic dusty rose / palo de rosa `#B76E79`
- Palette: dusty rose, white, black, soft warm off-white only as surface
- Typography direction: refined display serif for headings similar to Marcellus, modern sans for UI/body similar to Manrope
- Use real photography style: professional portraits, beauty studio, brow/lip results, training environment
- No abstract blobs, orbs, bokeh, gradient text, generic flower decorations or heavy beige theme

### Business Context

Cejas Internacionales has a physical studio in **Cali, Colombia** and offers appointments by availability in several cities:

- Colombia: Cali, Valle del Cauca; Restrepo, Valle del Cauca
- Spain: Madrid; Palma de Mallorca; Puerto de Sagunto, Valencia
- Switzerland: Geneva

Do not present Spain or Switzerland as fixed physical offices. They are appointment/jornada locations by availability.

### Website Goals

The website should:

- Present Xiomara as an international specialist and trainer
- Show services by market without mixing availability
- Highlight prices, appointment duration and result duration where available
- Show downloadable PDFs for service catalogs and training courses
- Drive all conversion to WhatsApp
- Show a beautiful animated editorial map with Cali as the physical studio and other cities as availability-based appointment locations
- Be bilingual: Spanish and international English
- Be SEO-safe from the design: crawlable text, clear page hierarchy, clean routes, image stability

### Required Pages / Screens

Design the main experience and enough sections to infer the system:

1. Home
2. Services by market
3. Service detail
4. Results gallery
5. Jornadas / international locations map
6. Training / courses
7. Course detail
8. About Xiomara
9. Contact
10. Downloads

### Home Structure

Create a first viewport with:

- Strong real-photo hero
- H1: Cejas Internacionales
- Supporting copy about micropigmentation, beauty and professional training, with studio in Cali and international appointment dates by availability
- Primary CTA: Contacta conmigo
- Secondary CTA: Ver servicios
- Small market selector: Colombia / España-Europa / Suiza
- Language switcher: ES / EN

Below the fold include:

- Market selector band
- Featured services
- Free photo assessment block
- Animated editorial map
- Real results mosaic
- About Xiomara teaser
- Training teaser
- Contact block

### UI Rules

- Buttons should be natural width, not full card width unless mobile requires it
- Avoid nested cards
- Avoid endless identical cards
- No carousel as primary content
- Use tabs/segmented controls for markets and categories
- Use compact badges for market, duration and currency
- Use tables only on desktop; use responsive data cards on mobile
- Mobile-first and elegant
- Touch targets at least 44px
- Language switcher should feel integrated, not noisy
- WhatsApp CTA should open a chooser with Colombia and Spain/Europe/Switzerland

### Map Direction

Design a custom editorial map, not a Google Maps iframe.

- Cali is the main physical studio pin
- Restrepo, Madrid, Palma de Mallorca, Puerto de Sagunto and Geneva are appointment-by-availability pins
- Use subtle dusty rose arcs from Cali to appointment cities
- On hover/tap, show a panel with city, country, type and WhatsApp CTA
- On mobile, provide a list-first accessible version with a compact map

### Services

Markets:

- Colombia: COP, Cali studio, includes broader service catalog such as brows, lips, lashes, waxing, nails, makeup/hair where applicable
- Spain/Europe: EUR, appointment dates by availability
- Switzerland: CHF, Geneva by availability

Do not mix services between markets.

### Training

Show professional trainings and masterclasses:

- Micropigmentation of brows
- Lip micropigmentation and neutralization
- Brow lamination
- Lash lifting
- Henna brows

Each course card/detail should support:

- Duration
- Modality
- Price by market if available
- Includes
- Download PDF
- CTA to WhatsApp for dates, seats and modality

### Bilingual Requirement

Show the design as bilingual-ready:

- Language switcher ES/EN
- Layout must handle longer English text
- Page metadata and route structure should be considered
- Spanish is source, English must sound professionally localized

### Style Keywords

Premium editorial beauty, precise catalog, international specialist, warm clinical trust, polished training academy, refined dusty rose accents, high whitespace discipline, clear conversion, real results.

### Avoid

- Ecommerce layout
- Fake booking form
- Fake Spain/Switzerland office
- Generic "transform your beauty" copy
- Full-width card buttons everywhere
- Overdecorated pink gradients
- Beige spa template
- AI-looking bento grid
- Stock illustrations
- Carousel-dependent content

Create a complete visual concept with desktop and mobile direction, key sections, component style, interactions and motion notes.
