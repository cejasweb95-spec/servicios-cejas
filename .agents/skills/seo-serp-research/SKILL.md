# SEO SERP Research

Use after `cejas-internacionales-guardrails`.

## Purpose

SEO research must happen before final copy, not after. Use current SERPs and competitor pages to decide:

- Page targets.
- Search intent.
- Headings.
- Content gaps.
- Internal links.
- Schema opportunities.
- Proof/trust elements.

## Free/Low-Cost Workflow

1. Search target queries manually or via web search.
2. Record top organic pages and their intent.
3. Separate directories/marketplaces from clinics/studios/academies.
4. Extract headings, content sections, pricing treatment, FAQ, CTAs, schema hints.
5. Compare against our confirmed data.
6. Build content brief for each page.
7. Avoid copying wording.

## Tools

- Google Search.
- Google Search Console after launch.
- PageSpeed Insights.
- Rich Results Test.
- Schema.org validator.
- Ahrefs Webmaster Tools free if site ownership is verified.
- Semrush free limits/manual checks.
- DinoRANK if user has account; not assumed free.
- Screaming Frog free up to 500 URLs.

- Do not invent Spain/Switzerland physical offices.
- Do not publish unconfirmed FAQ claims.
- Do not copy competitor text.
